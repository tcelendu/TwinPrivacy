SPDX identifiers are embedded in Python files and licence notices or supplied
through per-file `<filename>.license` sidecar files for formats in which a
comment could invalidate machine-readable data. The `test_publication.py`
suite checks the repository's file-level licensing metadata.

Ownership and attribution must be reviewed by the person or institution
releasing the source. The repository does not attribute software to an
unconfirmed individual and contains no third-party clinical data.

## AI-assisted development disclosure

OpenAI ChatGPT was used to assist with initial research-software design,
code development and revision, construction of generated demonstration
records, automated-test drafting, technical documentation, and preparation
of analysis and reporting workflows. Automated verification and computational
reproduction are provided for independent scrutiny. AI assistance does not
substitute for expert peer review or confer scientific validation.

The accountable applicant or repository maintainer is responsible for
confirming code functionality, evidential statements, authorship, licensing,
and any disclosures required by the funding application. This disclosure
may need updating if further tools are used.

## Dataset integrity and privacy

The released dataset is generated from a declared probabilistic model, with
no study participants or real patient data. Its clinical context fields do
not establish diagnoses or empirical epidemiology. Any subsequent use of
clinical records requires appropriate consent/legal basis, ethics review,
security safeguards, and fresh licence and provenance assessment.
