Evaluation design

Four modeled clinical attributes, sixteen weighted profile states, three recipient roles and eight ordered event templates per clinical profile. The cohort and all model parameters are generated or specified for software validation. No patient records were collected or analyzed.

Scientific positioning

The software combines established information-theoretic accounting with recipient-specific state, preference review, a patient-readable report and hash-linked receipts. None of these individual ideas is claimed as a new privacy theorem. An independently demonstrated advance beyond established audit and quantitative-information-flow approaches remains a substantive research goal.
